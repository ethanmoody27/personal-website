About

My path runs from studying finance and marketing at Penn State to serving as a Marine Corps Infantry Officer, learning full-stack development at Fullstack Academy, earning an M.S. in Product Management at Carnegie Mellon, and working as a product manager at two early-stage fintech startups. That mix put me at the intersection of business, leadership, operations, and technology.

Today I work in Product Operations and Business Operations at Bloomfield Robotics. My focus is simple: deeply understand the customer's world, find their real problems, and help the team deliver solutions that actually matter.

Outside of work, my world revolves around my family and skateboarding.
