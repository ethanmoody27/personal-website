02 — About

Business, leadership, and technology, in one path.

I started out studying finance and marketing at Penn State, then led Marines as an Infantry Officer. After the Corps I learned full-stack development at Fullstack Academy, earned an M.S. in Product Management at Carnegie Mellon, and worked as a product manager at two early-stage fintech startups.

That mix is how I work today: get deep into the customer's world, find the problem that actually matters, and get a cross-functional team aligned and moving on it.
